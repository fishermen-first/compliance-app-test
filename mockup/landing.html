How work moves

A compliance lifecycle that matches the office-to-vessel handoff.

Draft

Create the event

Add due date, vessel, owner, reminder rules, recurrence, and document links.

Active

Track the deadline

The office can see what is coming due and what needs prep before reminders go out.

Waiting on Vessel

Request response

Vessel contacts receive clear reminders and can respond when the item is handled.

Office Review

Confirm and close

The office verifies the response, updates notes or links, and marks the item complete.
